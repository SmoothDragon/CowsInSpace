# Flying Cow — Character Description

Mascot and box-cover hero for **Cows in Space**. Reference art: `CowsInSpace-box-cover.png`. Full-body character sheet: `CowsInSpace-flying-cow-fullbody.png`. Laser etch art (transparent grayscale): `CowsInSpace-flying-cow-etch.png` — preview on white: `CowsInSpace-flying-cow-etch-on-white.png`.

Use this document for 3D cow pieces (`spherical_cow.stl`), illustration, iconography, and vendor briefs.

---

## Overview

A **spherical Holstein cow** in a clear bubble helmet, designed for a bright, arcade-style space aesthetic (kart-racer energy, family-friendly). The body reads as a **ball with cow parts attached** — not a conventional cow stretched into a sphere.

---

## Silhouette & proportions

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Body** | Nearly a perfect sphere; black-and-white patches wrap the curved surface |
| **Head** | Small, nestled into the top of the sphere; face carries most of the personality |
| **Legs** | Four **short, stubby legs** with black hooves, tucked close to the body (zero-g pose) |
| **Tail** | Short tail with a **black tuft** at the tip |

---

## Color & markings

### Table pieces (3D print)

- **Base:** Solid **white** body for all cow miniatures
- **Holstein patches:** Irregular **black** splotches on body and head (same look as box art, simplified for scale)
- **Cow ID:** **Black pips on a white patch** on the back — standard die layouts with **1–6 pips** (no player colors)
- Pip count alone disambiguates the six cows; patches add Holstein character but vary slightly per sculpt
- Face, helmet, and hooves stay readable at miniature scale

### Illustration & box art

- **Base:** White with large, irregular **black Holstein patches** on body and head
- **Accents:** Pink snout and inner ears; tan/cream horns; black hooves and tail tuft
- Patches should stay **simple and bold** — readable at miniature scale and from across the table

---

## Face

- **Eyes:** Large, round, brown with strong white highlights — cheerful and wide-awake
- **Snout:** Round pink muzzle with two darker nostrils
- **Mouth:** Open, happy smile
- **Horns:** Small, curved, cream-colored
- **Ears:** Small; black outside, pink inside

---

## Space gear

- **Helmet:** Clear **glass bubble** fully enclosing the head; soft reflections, no opaque visor
- **Collar:** Thick **metallic silver** ring at the neck; small **glowing blue** lights or rivets (life-support / suit detail)

---

## Pose & motion

- **Flying** in zero gravity — body slightly tilted, legs tucked
- **Speed trails** behind the cow: blue, purple, orange, yellow — playful warp/orbit, not violent
- Mood: **joyful, adventurous, confident** — “racing through space,” not scared or goofy

---

## Style notes (3D print & future art)

- **Cel-shaded / polished 3D cartoon** — smooth surfaces, clean shapes, saturated color
- Must remain recognizable as the **spherical cow** from `spherical_cow.stl` and the card icon (`CowsInSpace.png`)
- Avoid realistic fur, horror, or mean expressions
- At miniature scale: emphasize **eyes, snout, helmet bubble, and patch contrast**

---

## Role in the brand

- **Box-cover hero** and public face of *Cows in Space*
- Anchor for cow piece design, hub art, rules/icons, and any future character illustration
- Hex motif belongs in the **environment** (moon, stars, board) — keep the cow itself clean and iconic
