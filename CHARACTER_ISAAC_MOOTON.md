## Overview

This belongs to the same character universe as the cow described in CHARACTER.md. 
All characteristics should be as similar as possible except for the named differences.

## Differences

Name: Isaac Mooton
Role in the brand: Isaac will introduce his laws of moo-tion, which will be rules for how cows move in the game.
Background: 
Isaac is a brilliant cow inspired by Isaac Newton.
He should wear a professor's cap as well as a hairpiece similar to Newton's.
Mooton is the **3-spot cow** (three laws of moo-tion) for game identity, but **illustration should not emphasize the 3-pip back** — keep pips subtle or out of frame; the mortarboard, wig, and classroom read as Isaac.

## Color & markings

### Illustration & box art

- **Base:** White with **multiple** large, irregular **black Holstein patches** on body (several distinct splotches wrapping the sphere — classic Holstein, not a single blotch)
- **Accents:** Pink snout; black hooves and tail tuft (ears/horns hidden under wig)

## Face

- **Eyes:** Large, round, brown with strong white highlights — cheerful and wide-awake
- **Snout:** Round pink muzzle with two darker nostrils
- **Mouth:** Open, happy smile
- **Horns / ears:** Present on the base design but **hidden under the Newton wig** for this character

---

## Pose & motion - These elements override the defaults if they conflict
- Classroom only: No space helmet bubble and collar, just wig and graduation cap.
- Cap type: Mortarboard hat, black.
- Wig: grey and curled, Newton style. Ears and horns covered.
- **Teaching** in front of a chalk board.
- Mood: **happy, lecturing, goofy**
- **Signature prop:** A **pointer stick** (thin wooden teacher’s pointer) held in a hoof, pointing at the chalkboard. Optional chalk tray / apple on desk — secondary.

## Chalkboard:
Has the following rules written:
   * A cow move starts by selecting a movement direction and ends by hitting another cow or wall.
   * A cow in motion moves in a straight line, unless acted upon by another cow.
   * A cow passing by cow (ignore walls) may enter to orbit around the cow, exiting in any of the six possible directions. Orbits can be chained together. A move is complete when the cow stops by hitting a wall or another cow.

Short art labels (for illustration): **1. Start & Stop** · **2. Straight Line** · **3. Orbits**

## Style notes (3D print & future art)

- **Cel-shaded / polished 3D cartoon** — smooth surfaces, clean shapes, saturated color
- Must remain recognizable as the **spherical cow** from `spherical_cow.stl` and the card icon (`CowsInSpace.png`)
- Avoid realistic fur, horror, mean expressions, human hands, or a space helmet on Isaac
- At miniature scale: emphasize **eyes, snout, mortarboard silhouette, grey wig, and patch contrast** (pips secondary)
- Hero illustration focal points: **face, mortarboard, wig, pointing stick → chalkboard**
